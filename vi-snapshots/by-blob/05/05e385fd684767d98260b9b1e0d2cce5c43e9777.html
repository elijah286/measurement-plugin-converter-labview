Find Exact Peak.vi

Finds an accurate approximation to the peak reponse and frequency location using the window coefficients and a binary search algorithm. The best guess is initialized to the center of the frequency bins and is moved within the search loop until the window reponses at the bin edges closely approximate the observed magnitude responses of the original spectrum.
