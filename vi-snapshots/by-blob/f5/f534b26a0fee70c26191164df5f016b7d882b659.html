ADC SNR_Sweep.vi

[AutoDocStart]Performs crystal accuracy measurements. Measures the offset from ideal frequency. Primarily used for tuning XOSC capacitors and verifying XOSC trims.

Sweeping parameters: operating frequency, OSC override value and VDDR level

Results: Offset frequency in ppm[AutoDocEnd]
